# Privacy Policy for Campus OS for Obsidian

*Last updated: November 2026*

**Campus OS** is an open-source community plugin created by **Nelxson2099** for Obsidian (https://obsidian.md).

---

## 1. Local-First Philosophy & No Tracking
Campus OS operates strictly on a **local-first** model:
- All academic data, courses, assignments, grades, and notes are saved **exclusively on your local machine** within your Obsidian vault (`data.json`).
- There are **no external servers, databases, or intermediary trackers**. Your personal data never leaves your computer.

---

## 2. No External Network Requests
Campus OS does not make network calls or transmit analytics, telemetry, or user behavior to anyone.

---

## 3. Data Control
You have complete control over your academic data:
- Modify or delete courses and tasks at any time from within Obsidian.
- Deleting the plugin or removing the folder completely purges all plugin data from your system.
